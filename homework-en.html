Calculations, observations and data analysis

  • Geostrophic calculations and the Gulf Stream | Three bilingual questionsACC pressure gradient, volume transport and dynamic-height difference, followed by a four-panel reading of Gulf Stream density and geostrophic velocity.
  • Five ocean-dynamics questions | Inertia, PV and SverdrupOriginal classroom prompts and detailed bilingual answers, with inertial-period calculations, force and reference-velocity diagrams, PV factors and the Ekman–Sverdrup derivation.
  • Geostrophic currents: from surface slopes to the ocean interiorFrom pressure gradients and Coriolis acceleration to geostrophic velocity, sea-level slope and thermal wind. Includes hemisphere diagrams, an interactive lab and a complete worked example.
  • EOF explained | Real SST, projection and reconstructionLearn EOF from zero with NOAA ERSSTv5: 564 months and 1,626 ocean cells. Follow preprocessing, projection, SVD, spatial modes and reconstruction. Download bilingual figures, data and Python code.
  • Mixed layers and ocean structure | 7 questionsFrom mixed-layer depth to the thermocline: seven classroom questions with sourced reference explanations.
  • Ocean surface heat budget | 2 questionsControls, signs and typical scales of shortwave, longwave, latent and sensible heat fluxes. Exact extrema await the assigned maps.
