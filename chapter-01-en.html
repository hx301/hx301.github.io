Descriptive Physical Oceanography and Scales

Chapter 1 · Textbook Ch.1 §1.1–1.2 · Talley et al. (2011), 6th ed.

Spatial scales: 1 mm – 10,000 km
Time scales: seconds – millennia

Textbook Ch.1 §1.2, Fig. 1.2 (ocean time and space scales)

My explanation

The textbook opens with a scale diagram: waves at seconds–metres, tides at hours–hundreds of kilometres, circulation at years–millennia and thousands of kilometres. The point is not to memorize the ranges but to decide which terms can be neglected and how densely to sample.

Link to class

Session 1 used \(\mathrm{Ro}=U/(fL)\) to turn “scale” into a computable criterion; the textbook gives the panorama, the class gives the test. Difference: the textbook emphasizes the descriptive tradition; the class emphasizes testable questions.
