Mixed-Layer Heat Budget

Chapter 5 · Textbook Ch.5 §5.4–5.5 · Talley et al. (2011), 6th ed.

\[\rho_0c_p h\frac{\partial T}{\partial t}=Q_{\mathrm{net}}-\text{advection}-\text{entrainment}\]

Textbook Ch.5 §5.4–5.5

My explanation

\(Q_{\mathrm{net}}\) is the sum of shortwave, longwave, latent, and sensible heat; mixed-layer temperature also responds to advection, entrainment, and layer-thickness changes. A 20 m layer absorbing \(100 W/m^{2}\) for a day warms by about 0.105 K; a 100 m layer by about 0.021 K.

Link to class

Session 5 worked through the control volume and sign convention; the textbook emphasizes conservation and spatial distribution. Difference: the class calculates first; the textbook discusses global distributions later.

My extension

OAFlux, ERA5, WHOI heat-flux products; NOAA PMEL upper-ocean heat balance.
