T–S Diagrams and Water Masses

Chapter 4 · Textbook Ch.4 §4.2–4.7 · Talley et al. (2011), 6th ed.

Mean temperature 3.5 °C · Mean salinity 34.6
Three quarters of the volume: 0–6 °C, 34–35

Textbook Ch.4 §4.2–4.7

My explanation

A T–S diagram puts the two variables together: one point is one water type, a mixing line is straight, and isopycnals give the density background. Water-mass identification also needs oxygen, nutrients, density level, and spatial continuity.

Link to class

Session 4 used the subtropical salinity maximum to explain E−P; the textbook adds oxygen, nutrients, and water age. Difference: the textbook treats water masses as a multi-tracer problem; the class starts with T–S.

My extension

Argo, WOA18, NOAA NCEI water-mass data; \(O_{2}\)/nitrate tracers.
